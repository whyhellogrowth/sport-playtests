SPORT Playtest Updates

Program

SPORT Playtest Updates, run by Miles Cole. Text messages about SPORT playtest invitations, scheduling, confirmations and updates. You can answer our questions by replying with a number or Y/N.

How you join

Provide your mobile number and explicitly opt in to SPORT text updates, either through a sign-up form with SMS disclosures or by directly requesting SMS/text updates in an email or text reply. Providing a phone number alone does not establish SMS consent. Consent is optional and is not a condition of any purchase.

Frequency and cost

Message frequency varies, typically up to 6 messages per month. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt out and help

Reply STOP at any time; you'll get one confirmation and no further messages. Reply HELP for help, or email milescolemusic@gmail.com.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

See our Privacy Policy. We do not share your mobile number or messaging consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
