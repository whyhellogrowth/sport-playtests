SPORT: a new balance-based sport

We're playtesting a new sport around Los Angeles and looking for people to play.

Sign up through the QR code on our flyers or through our Instagram/Facebook ads. We'll text or email you about upcoming playtests. You can reply with a number to tell us whether you're in, maybe, or want to hear about the next one.

Text messages

Our sign-up forms show this next to the phone number field:

By entering your phone number, you agree to receive text messages from SPORT (Miles Cole) about playtest invitations and updates. Msg frequency varies (up to 6/mo). Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. See our Privacy Policy and SMS Terms.

Giving a phone number is optional; you can give an email instead.
