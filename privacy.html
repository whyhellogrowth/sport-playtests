Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026

SPORT is a new balance-based sport being playtested in Los Angeles. Playtests are organized by Miles Cole ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

When you sign up through our flyer QR code form or our Instagram/Facebook sign-up form, we collect what you give us. That can include your name, mobile phone number, email address, general area of LA, availability, and optional details such as age range and gender. We also keep the replies you send to our texts and emails.

How we use it

We use your information only to run SPORT playtests. That means inviting you to playtests, coordinating schedules and locations, sending confirmations and reminders, answering your questions, and improving future playtests.

Text messaging

If you give us your mobile number, you agree to receive text messages from SPORT (Miles Cole) about playtest invitations, scheduling and updates.

  • Message frequency varies, typically up to 6 messages per month.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to opt out at any time. Reply HELP for help, or email milescolemusic@gmail.com.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We may share information with service providers who help us operate the playtests and send messages, such as our text messaging and email providers. They may only use it to provide that service. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are excluded from all other sharing and will not be shared with any third parties.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information while you're on our playtest list. To be removed or to have your information deleted, reply STOP to any text, reply "remove me" to any email, or email milescolemusic@gmail.com.

Age

Playtests are for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. Questions: milescolemusic@gmail.com.
